# 0001 - Reading a topo map

## Goal
Read basic contour patterns and estimate distances using a map scale.

## What the learner demonstrated
The learner correctly identified a steep hillside from tightly spaced contours and calculated a short map distance from a representative fraction. They could also find north on a map with a printed north arrow.

## Evidence
During a guided practice task, the learner explained that a fixed elevation change spread across a shorter horizontal map distance means steeper terrain. They checked a distance conversion using units rather than guessing.

## What to revisit
The learner still needs practice distinguishing the north references used on a map and compass, and checking a route against terrain while moving.

## Suggested next step
Practice setting and following bearings from a topographic map, then introduce conservative off-trail route planning.
