# Mission

## Why
I want to navigate confidently on day hikes when a marked trail is unclear or visibility becomes limited. I want to understand what a map and compass can and cannot tell me rather than relying only on a phone.

## What success looks like
I can read a topographic map, take and follow an appropriate bearing, check my progress against terrain, and plan a conservative route with clear checkpoints and a way to turn back.

## Constraints
Use concrete terrain examples, short exercises, and explanations appropriate for a beginner with some hiking experience. Explain safety limits without substituting personalized medical advice.

## Out of scope
Technical GPS algorithms, mountaineering route-finding on exposed terrain, and individualized expedition or medical decisions.
